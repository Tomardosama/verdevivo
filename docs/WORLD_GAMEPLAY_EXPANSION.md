# TRush world and gameplay expansion

This update expands the forest play space from 60 x 60 to 120 x 120 world units.

## Included
- 64 procedurally placed trees, 360 grass tufts and 95 randomized rocks.
- Four waste visual categories (plastic bottle, aluminum can, paper/cardboard and glass container) with differentiated recycling rewards.
- Recycling station with sorting-bin visuals and a short scale pulse on deposit.
- Short FOV feedback tween when collecting waste.
- Wildlife actor refinements: facial details, species silhouettes, idle movement and bounded wandering around each animal's habitat.
- Progression markers for Arroyo (35%) and Borde Salvaje (70%), persisted as unlocked zone milestones.
- Rounded HUD panels, buttons and timed notifications; mission text, bestiary, pause/menu panel, session stats and restoration achievements.

## Current limitations
- Forest assets are procedural placeholder meshes; these are not production-quality scanned or hand-authored models.
- The new zones are progression-marked areas in the expanded forest. Physical barriers, separate level streaming and distinct biome gameplay are future work.
- Sound assets and final character animations are not included in this pass.
- Automated CI can validate project import and startup; interactive play, visual quality and performance still require a local Godot run.
